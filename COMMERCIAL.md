# Commercial Licence

> **Draft.** The terms of the Commercial Licence aren't written yet. Have a lawyer draft the agreement before any licence is sold.

Latiprimere is free under the [Community Licence](LICENSE) for small organisations, individuals, non-profits and education, open-source projects, evaluation and development, and indirect use. Section 1 of the licence gives the exact conditions.

## Who needs a Commercial Licence

Any organisation whose annual gross revenue, including its affiliates, was US$1,000,000 or more in its most recent financial year, and that uses Latiprimere in production. Production means any use except evaluating Latiprimere or developing and testing software with it. For example:

- running Latiprimere, or software that includes it, in the organisation's own operations;
- distributing software that includes Latiprimere to customers or others, whether for payment or free of charge;
- providing a service that uses Latiprimere.

The exception is use in a project published under an open-source licence (Community Licence, condition 1c).

An organisation that grows past the limit has 90 days to get a Commercial Licence.

**One licence covers one company.** In a group, the revenue limit counts the whole group, but each company in it that uses Latiprimere in production needs its own licence.

## Price

**US$250, €225 or £190 per company, per year.**

Prices are shown in euros in the eurozone, pounds in the UK and US dollars elsewhere, but you can pay in any of the three.

Prices exclude VAT and sales tax, which are added at checkout where they apply.

## What it gives you

- **Production use** for organisations of any size.
- **Every developer and product** in the company.
- **All updates** while the licence is active.
- **Email support,** as well as GitHub issues.
- **Invoice and VAT handled for you.**

Everyone, with either licence, can use [GitHub issues](https://github.com/bananocraft-miner82/Latiprimere/issues) and the documentation at [latiprimere.com](https://latiprimere.com), which is still being written.

## Renewal

A licence lasts a year. To keep using Latiprimere in production after that, renew within 30 days of the end date. Otherwise, stop using it in production. Evaluation and development stay free under the Community Licence.

## Refunds

If a licence isn't right for you, ask for a refund within 30 days of buying it.

## How to get one

Commercial Licences aren't on sale yet. They will be sold at [latiprimere.com](https://latiprimere.com) through Paddle, which acts as the seller: it takes the payment, issues the invoice, and handles VAT and sales tax.

Until then, to ask about a licence, or about anything else in the licence, email [sales@latiprimere.com](mailto:sales@latiprimere.com).

Organisations that want to contribute to Latiprimere (see [CLA.md](CLA.md)) can use the same address.
