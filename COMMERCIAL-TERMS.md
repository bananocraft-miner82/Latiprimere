## Changes to the Standard Terms

These changes to the Standard Terms apply, by section number.

### The licence

1. **Business use only (new).** Customer enters into the Agreement for its business, not as a consumer.
2. **Licence (1.1).** The licence covers the Permitted Uses. Customer may copy and modify the Software as they need. Customer may also let the recipients of its products and services use the Software, but only as part of those products and services. Customer may not distribute the Software on its own, or as part of a library, SDK or other development tool that lets others use the Software's programming interfaces.
3. **User accounts (1.2).** The Software has no user accounts, so 1.2 applies only to any account Customer has with Provider's reseller.
4. **Usage Data and machine learning (1.3 and 1.4).** The Software collects no Usage Data and sends nothing to Provider. Only the Feedback part of 1.3 applies; 1.4 doesn't apply.
5. **Updates (1.6).** Updates are every version of the Software that Provider releases during the Subscription Period, including new major versions. Customer doesn't have to install Updates. The exclusions in 5.3 for not installing them still apply.

### Restrictions and payment

6. **Restrictions (2.1).** The source code of the Software is public. So the restrictions on reverse engineering and decompiling, on copying and modifying, and on publishing performance evaluations don't apply. The restriction on providing or distributing the Software applies only as change 2 describes. The other restrictions in 2.1 apply.
7. **Suspension (2.2).** Section 2.2 doesn't apply. If a renewal payment fails, Customer has 30 days from the end of the Subscription Period to pay. The Agreement continues during those 30 days. If Customer hasn't paid by then, the Agreement ends.
8. **Payment and taxes (3).**
   - Customer pays through Provider's reseller, which sells the licence as reseller and merchant of record. The reseller's own terms, accepted at checkout, govern payment, invoices, VAT and sales tax. Those terms replace 3.2 to 3.6.
   - The reseller collects VAT and sales tax under 3.4 and handles invoicing.
   - Fees are in the currency Customer chose at checkout, not U.S. Dollars.
9. **Refunds (3.1).** Fees aren't non-refundable as 3.1 says. Customer may ask for a full refund of a payment, including a renewal payment, within 30 days of making it. On a refund, the Subscription Period that payment was for ends, and with it the Agreement.

### Term and termination

10. **Renewal and cancellation (4.1).** The Agreement renews automatically at the end of each Subscription Period, unless one party gives notice by its Non-Renewal Notice Date. Customer may give notice by cancelling at the reseller, or by email to Provider. After notice, the Agreement ends at the end of the current Subscription Period. Apart from change 9, there is no refund for the rest of that Subscription Period.
11. **Effect of termination (4.5).** When the Agreement ends, Customer follows the Deletion Procedure. Customer's rights under the Community Licence aren't affected by the Agreement ending. Recipients of copies of Customer's products distributed while the Agreement was in force may keep using them as part of those products.
12. **Security updates after the Agreement ends (new; survives under 4.6).** Even after the Agreement ends, Customer may update the copies of its products it distributed while the Agreement was in force with Security Updates of the Software. Customer may distribute those updated copies to the recipients of the original copies.
    - **A Security Update** is a later version of the Software in the same line, such as `11.0.x`, that Provider releases to fix a vulnerability, as its security advisory says.
    - **This lasts** until the end of the support period of that line, as Provider's security policy (SECURITY.md) states.
    - **It doesn't let Customer** use the Software in production in its own operations, or distribute it in new copies of its products to anyone else.

### Warranties, liability and indemnity

13. **Warranty remedy (5.4).** If Provider can't repair or replace defective Software within a reasonable time, Customer may end the Agreement. Provider then refunds the Fees for the current Subscription Period.
14. **Liability (7.4).** The cap and the damages waiver don't limit liability for damage caused by a party's intent or deliberate recklessness, or that of its management.
15. **Indemnity (8).** There are no Covered Claims, so Provider doesn't defend or indemnify Customer against claims by others, including claims that the Software infringes their intellectual property. The disclaimer in 6.1 includes non-infringement.

### Other terms

16. **Confidentiality (9 and 11.7).** This Cover Page is public, so its terms aren't Confidential Information. That a particular Customer has an Agreement is still Confidential Information, as are its order details.
17. **Entire agreement (10.1).** The Agreement doesn't replace the reseller's terms for the purchase, or the Community Licence. Where Customer meets a condition of the Community Licence, it may use the Software under either licence.
18. **Changes to these terms (10.2).** Provider may change this Cover Page for a later Subscription Period by notice to Customer at least 30 days before that period starts. If Customer doesn't cancel before then, the new terms apply from the start of that period.
19. **Customer's name and logo (10.8).** Provider may name Customer, or use its name or logo, only with Customer's prior written consent. Email counts as written consent.
20. **Notices (10.9).** Email to the Notice Address is enough for any notice under the Agreement.
21. **Export controls and sanctions (10.12).** The export control and sanctions laws that apply to either party, including those of the European Union and the Netherlands, replace the references to U.S. laws. Provider may still end the Agreement immediately to comply with them.
22. **Language (new).** The Agreement is in English. A translation is for convenience only.
